1. Introduction

Welcome to the Asha Grill House App! This application replaces heavy, messy paper ledgers (Khata) with a smart, digital mobile solution. It is designed to easily store, search, update, and manage all your customer data in one secure place.

  • Store new customer details, phone numbers, and addresses.
  • Add due amounts with proper receipt images.
  • Maintain a date-wise history of customer payments.
  • Take exact site measurements directly on your phone.
  • Track daily shop expenses efficiently.
  • Generate powerful monthly or yearly business reports.

2. Buttom Navigation Pages

Main Navigation & Home

When you open the app, you will see 4 main navigation buttons at the bottom: Home, Hisab, Maf, and Khoroch. Clicking any button takes you to that specific section.

On the Home page, there is a QR Scanner to accept payments (you receive an instant message). There is also a Gallery section to browse images of grill designs.

Hisab (Accounts)

Click "Add" on top to fill a form for a new customer (with due and receipt images). Once saved, you can view their details, send SMS reminders, and manage their transactions date-wise.

Maf (Measurements)

Enter customer details to log measurements via two options:

  • Form: Enter data and click an image to save.
  • Canvas: Draw sketches manually on the screen.

You can also add advance money here and save it directly to the customer's page.

Khoroch & Orders

Khoroch: Add daily expenses and view them date-wise.
Orders: Place material orders by selecting items and sending them directly via an image or message.

3. Download & Install

To install the app on your mobile device, please follow these simple steps:

  • Download the provided .apk file to your device.
  • Tap the file and click "Install" (Allow "Unknown Sources" if prompted).
  • Wait for the process to finish, then click "Open".

4. Initial Setup

When launching for the first time, please grant the necessary permissions (Camera, Microphone, Storage). To restore older data, navigate to the side drawer, click "Import Database," and select your previous backup file.

5. Backend Architecture

The app relies entirely on a highly secure, offline SQLite Database. It stores Customers, Transactions, Measurements, and Orders locally on your device ensuring blazing fast speeds and no internet dependency. You have full control to export and backup your database anytime.

6. Conclusion

The Asha Grill House App is the ultimate digital tool designed to replace traditional paper Khata. With advanced features like photo receipts, drawing canvases, and automated SMS, managing your grill shop has never been easier or more secure.
