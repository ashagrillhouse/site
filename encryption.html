How End-to-End Encryption Works

End-to-End Encryption ensures only you and your receiver can read messages.

🔐 What is End-to-End Encryption?

End-to-End Encryption is a security method where data is encrypted on the sender’s device and decrypted only on the receiver’s device. The message remains encrypted while traveling through servers, networks, and the internet.

Even if hackers, internet providers, or the platform itself intercept the message, they will only see unreadable encrypted data.

📡 Message Flow Visualization

This is how your message is encrypted between you and your receiver. Only you and your receiver can read the message as plain text. No one, even our company, can read it. We can see that your message is delivered, but it is totally encrypted and not readable. This is how your message stays totally safe, private, personalized, and secret.

🧠 How Encryption Technically Works

This is how our workflow works. We do not send your message data to other servers. We save your message data to your local storage for extra safety. And your chatbot automatically generates the reply and responds to you. It also uses encryption techniques to store the data. If any attacker steals the message, they only get encrypted data in an unreadable format. Without the key, it is totally useless.

Each user has two keys:

  • Public Key: Shared with others to encrypt messages
  • Private Key: Stored securely on device to decrypt messages

Example encrypted message:

9f3A@#Xz12!aQ0=KpLmWz8T9+Zx

Only the correct private key can convert this encrypted data back into readable text.

🛡 Why End-to-End Encryption is Important

✔ Prevents spying and surveillance
✔ Protects personal conversations
✔ Ensures data integrity
✔ Builds user trust

This is why modern apps like WhatsApp, Signal, and secure banking platforms rely heavily on E2EE.
